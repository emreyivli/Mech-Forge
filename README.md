## What is MechForge?

MechForge is a focused desktop application for mechanical and manufacturing engineers who need quick, reliable cross-section property calculations and strength analysis — without the overhead of a full CAD/FEA suite.

**Key capabilities:**
- 11 parametric cross-section geometries (rectangle, circle, I-beam, C-channel, L-angle, T-section, hollow profiles, and more)
- Stress envelope & safety-factor analysis for 4 beam loading scenarios
- 142 TS EN catalogue profiles built in
- 15 standard engineering materials
- 3D section preview with STL export
- Publication-quality PDF reports with full formula traceability
- Runs 100% offline — no cloud, no telemetry, no account required
- Turkish & English UI
